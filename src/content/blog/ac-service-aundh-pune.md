## Why AC Maintenance Is Important

Many people call an AC technician only when the air conditioner stops working. However, regular maintenance can help identify small issues before they become bigger problems.

Over time, dust can accumulate in air filters, indoor coils and other components. This buildup may reduce airflow and affect cooling performance.

Regular AC maintenance can help:

- **Maintain proper cooling:** Cleaning filters and coils helps air move properly through the AC.
- **Improve airflow:** Removing dust and dirt helps reduce airflow restrictions.
- **Prevent unexpected breakdowns:** Inspection may identify loose connections or early signs of faults.
- **Reduce unnecessary strain:** A properly maintained AC can operate more efficiently.
- **Maintain indoor comfort:** Regular cleaning helps keep your AC working properly during hot days.

## Common AC Problems Faced by Homeowners in Aundh

AC problems can occur because of regular usage, dust buildup, electrical issues or a lack of maintenance. Identifying the problem early can help avoid further damage.

| AC problem | Possible cause | Recommended action |

| AC not cooling properly | Dirty filters, blocked coils or refrigerant leakage | Inspect and clean the AC; diagnose if cooling remains poor |

| AC not starting | Electrical or component fault | Professional electrical troubleshooting |
| Weak airflow | Dirty filter or blower | Cleaning and airflow inspection |

## AC Services Available in Aundh, Pune

Professional AC technicians can help with different maintenance and repair requirements.

### 1. AC General Servicing

General servicing helps remove dust and dirt from important AC components. It may include cleaning the filters, checking the indoor unit, inspecting drainage and checking the overall performance of the AC.

Regular servicing can help maintain airflow and cooling performance. The exact tasks included can vary, so confirm the service checklist when booking.

### 2. AC Repair Service

If your AC is not cooling, making noise, leaking water or not turning on, a technician can inspect the unit and identify the fault.

AC repair may involve checking electrical connections, fan components, sensors, PCB or other parts depending on the problem. A diagnosis should be completed before replacement parts are recommended.

### 3. AC Deep Cleaning

Deep cleaning is useful when dust and dirt accumulate inside the AC. It may include cleaning the filters, coils, blower and other accessible components, depending on the unit and service scope.

Cleaning the AC properly can help improve airflow and remove accumulated dirt. Ask the technician which components are included in the cleaning.

### 4. AC Gas Leakage Inspection

If your AC is not cooling properly, refrigerant leakage may be one possible reason. However, low cooling can also happen because of dirty filters, blocked airflow or other faults.

A technician should inspect the system before recommending refrigerant filling. If a leak is found, it should be repaired before refrigerant is added. Simply topping up refrigerant without addressing the cause may lead to recurring problems.

### 5. AC Installation and Uninstallation

Proper installation is important for AC performance and long-term operation. Professional technicians can help install new air conditioners, uninstall existing units or relocate ACs when required.

Proper drainage, electrical connections and placement of indoor and outdoor units should be checked during installation.

## How to Maintain Your AC Between Services

You do not always need a technician for basic AC maintenance. A few simple habits can help keep your air conditioner in better condition:

- Clean or check the air filter regularly, following the manufacturer's instructions.
- Keep the indoor unit area free from dust and obstructions.
- Avoid setting the temperature extremely low unnecessarily.
- Keep doors and windows closed while the AC is running.
- Make sure the outdoor unit has sufficient airflow.
- Do not ignore unusual sounds, smells or water leakage.

If you notice electrical issues or problems inside the AC, avoid opening the unit yourself. Switch off the AC if it is safe to do so and arrange professional inspection.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How often should I service my AC in Aundh?

It is generally recommended to service your AC once or twice a year, depending on usage, dust levels and the condition of the unit. Follow the manufacturer's maintenance recommendations.

### 2. Why is my AC not cooling properly?

Dirty filters, blocked coils, airflow problems or refrigerant leakage can affect cooling. A technician can inspect the AC to identify the actual cause.

### 3. Does AC servicing include cleaning the filters?

Filter cleaning is commonly included in general AC servicing. However, the exact service checklist may vary between technicians, so confirm what is included.

### 4. Can regular AC servicing help prevent breakdowns?

Regular maintenance may help identify small problems, remove dust buildup and check important components, potentially reducing the chances of unexpected breakdowns.

### 5. When should I call an AC technician?

Contact a technician if your AC is not cooling, leaking water, making unusual noises, switching off frequently or failing to start. Avoid opening electrical components yourself.
